Hebrew Reader: three visual directions

The Hebrew text and the lookup for the word החמישי are real. The counts, streak, progress bars, review intervals, multiple-choice wrong answers and the "chamishi" spelling are placeholders.

A. Paper: a printed book. Warm page, serif Hebrew, one terracotta accent.

B. Night: an e-reader in the dark. Sans Hebrew, amber for what you looked up.

C. Bold: a game you want to open daily. Color blocks, big buttons, progress.
